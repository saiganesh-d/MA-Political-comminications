Programme Overview

The M.A. in Political Communication explores how politics, media, and public narratives shape contemporary societies.

The programme helps students understand:

  • The role of communication in politics, elections, governance, and democratic life
  • How journalism and media systems shape political discourse
  • Political strategy, campaigns, and public opinion dynamics
  • Digital platforms, data analytics, and their role in modern politics
  • Behavioural insights and emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence

Students engage with theory, case studies, campaign simulations, and practitioner-led modules to understand political communication across diverse media and political contexts.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the programme theoretical or practice-oriented?

It is both. The programme combines strong theoretical foundations with experiential learning, including case studies, simulations, campaign labs, and practitioner-led modules.

Is financial assistance or EMI available?

Yes. Easy EMI options are available to help you finance the programme conveniently.

Who should apply for this programme?

Graduates from any discipline who are interested in politics, media, communication, public policy, and campaigns.

What will I study in this programme?

You will study political communication, media systems, public opinion, campaign strategy, data analytics, and emerging technologies.

Do I receive a degree upon completion?

Yes. You receive an M.A. degree from O.P. Jindal Global University upon successful completion.
