Programme Overview

The M.A. in Political Communication explores how politics, media, and public narratives shape contemporary societies.

The programme helps students understand:

  • The role of communication in politics, elections, governance, and democratic life
  • How journalism and media systems shape political discourse
  • Political strategy, campaigns, and public opinion dynamics
  • Digital platforms, data analytics, and their role in modern politics
  • Behavioural insights and emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence

Students engage with theory, case studies, campaign simulations, and practitioner-led modules to understand political communication across diverse media and political contexts.
